# Kael-inspired articulated fixture

Original geometric SVG authored for AETHER-DOM Task 11 on 14 November 2026.
It is a new editable illustration, not traced from or derived from the existing
Kael or Vance sprites. The SVG and rig template are project-original work;
the direct-root group IDs bind the single source asset to the rig. Template
entity IDs are placeholders and must be remapped on import; SVG group IDs stay
unchanged. The entire SVG shares one source and rights status.
